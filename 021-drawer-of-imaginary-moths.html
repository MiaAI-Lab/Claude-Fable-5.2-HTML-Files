Moths That Were Never Caught

Edwin Mallory Hollis (1849–1921) kept, beside his true cabinet, a second one for the moths he was certain of and never managed to take. Each was drawn up, named, labelled and pinned with full ceremony. The Museum has not been able to fault his taxonomy, only his evidence.

Night attendants report that one specimen in every drawer is, now and then, found on the wrong pin. It should not be handled twice.
