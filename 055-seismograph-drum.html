How to Read the Record

The first sharp tremor is P, the push-and-pull wave that travels fastest; the second, larger and slower, is S, the shear wave. Count the seconds between them: each second of S − P puts the shock roughly nine kilometres farther off. The long rolling swell that follows, L, has come the slow way round the surface of the Earth, and its height tells the size of the shock.

Visitors are asked not to stamp. — E. Marrable, Observer
