INTERACTION MODEL
Scrolling is the main control. Each chapter's figure stays pinned while its text passes, and the scroll position within the chapter sets a target that the drawing eases toward, so folding, rolling, proofing and baking never jump and can be scrubbed backwards. In the oven chapter, pointing at or tapping the cross-section highlights the layer under the pointer, drops a tagged leader line on it and prints its name and a one-line definition beneath the figure; the three chips under the figure highlight all crust, all membranes or all butter lines and alveoli at once, and work from the keyboard. On the title spread, clicking or tapping the croissant (or pressing Enter or Space on it) tears off one end with a spring and a fall of crumbs, a second press tears the other, and a third bakes a new one. Contents links jump to chapters and table rows warm on hover. With reduced motion requested, the scroll-linked morphing is switched off: every figure shows its finished state and gains a previous/next stepper for moving through named stages, and entrances and steam are stilled.
