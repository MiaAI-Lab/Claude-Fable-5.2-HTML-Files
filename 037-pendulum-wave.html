Pendulum Wave

Cabinet of Arithmetic · Case 37

Fifteen pendulums, each a little shorter than the last. Let go together, they fall out of step, and in one minute, to the instant, fall back in.

Each pendulum is cut to make a whole number of swings in one minute: fifty-one for the longest, one more for each neighbour, sixty-five for the shortest. Let go together, each gains on the one beside it by exactly one-sixtieth of a swing every second, so the row slides out of step into waves, then quarters, thirds and halves. After sixty seconds every count is whole again, and the row has no choice but to meet.
