III

Le nez · the nose

Séverine Aubrac

Parfumeuse · formerly of the paper trade

Séverine Aubrac worked for eleven years as a paper conservator, which is to say she was paid to stop old things from smelling of anything at all.

One winter a small fire in a private reading room was put out with what was to hand, which was a pot of tea. She was called the next morning to assess the damage and stayed, by her own account, forty minutes longer than the work required.

No. 7 is those forty minutes. It took four years and 212 trials; she maintains that trial nine was already correct, and that the remainder were manners.

« Le papier se souvient de tout, surtout de ce qu’on lui a fait. »

Paper remembers everything, especially what was done to it.

L’objet · the object

un feu, un chêne.

Verre · glass
Hand-blown at the Verrerie de Val-d’Orne, a village of 311 people and one furnace. Walls six millimetres thick; no two flasks quite agree.
Contenance · volume
50 ml. Sufficient for one room and a year of evenings.
Bouchon · cap
Oak, turned, then blackened in an open fire and rubbed with beeswax. It will mark white gloves. This is intended.
Col · collar
Brass, gilded, engraved with the batch.
Concentration
Extrait, 28 %. Two sprays towards the curtains; the room does the rest.
