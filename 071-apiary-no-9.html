House rule

We do not make honey. We collect rent.

The tenants number forty thousand to a hive, work until their wings wear out, and have never once asked what we do with it.

How a bee gives directions

The waggle dance

  1. Straight up the comb means fly straight toward the sun.
  2. Turn the run forty degrees to the right of vertical and she means forty degrees to the right of the sun.
  3. The longer she waggles, the further you fly: about one second for every kilometre.
