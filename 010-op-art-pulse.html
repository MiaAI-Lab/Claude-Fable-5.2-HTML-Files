SIXROOMS OFOPTICAL PRESSURE

Nothing in these six rooms moves the way you will see it move. Each work is a pair of plain instructions — rings, a checkerboard, stripes, spokes, ruled lines, one disc — laid over one another in black and in white. The swelling, the shimmer, the greys and the faint colours that gather at the edges are manufactured behind your eyes. The screen only nudges. Stand close, then stand back.

AdvisoryThese works flicker. A calm version is shown if your device asks for reduced motion.
