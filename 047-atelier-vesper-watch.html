The workshop is the front room of a farmhouse above Les Brenassons, facing north-east so the light stays flat until noon. Colette Vesper-Aubry spent nineteen years finishing other people’s movements in the valley before she drew her own. Her nephew Basile joined in the third winter; he cuts the wheels and pinions, she does everything that gets looked at through a loupe.

A Heure Bleue takes about four weeks. Three of them are finishing. When a watch leaves, the bench is cleared, the next plate is clamped down, and that is the whole of our production planning.

We do not have a boutique. If you are passing, ring the bell twice — once sounds like the postman, and we let the postman wait.
