House reference · eleven grades

A Short Catalogue of Rain

Other cities have weather. Garrow has a filing system. Our counter staff grade every shower before recommending a remedy; touch a grade to see it fall.

  1. 01

    Mizzle

    Finer than drizzle, wetter than mist. You do not notice it until you are soaked.

    Umbrella: optional
  2. 02

    Smirr

    Rain that hangs about rather than falls. It has nowhere else to be.

    Umbrella: decorative
  3. 03

    Spitting

    Individual drops, sent ahead to see whether you will go back for a coat.

    Umbrella: carried, smugly
  4. 04

    Drizzle

    The civil service of rain: unhurried, thorough, and here until five.

    Umbrella: advised
  5. 05

    A steady soak

    The Garrow default. Gardeners call it useful. Nobody else calls it anything.

    Umbrella: yes
  6. 06

    Pelting

    Audible on the hat. Conversations are resumed indoors.

    Umbrella: yes, and hurry
  7. 07

    Stair-rods

    Straight down, in visible lines. Named by people who still had stair-rods.

    Umbrella: essential
  8. 08

    Sideways

    Arrives from the harbour, horizontally. The umbrella is held like a shield.

    Umbrella: aimed
  9. 09

    The kind that gets in at the cuffs

    No known defence. We sell a sleeve garter. It does not help.

    Umbrella: a gesture
  10. 10

    Cloudburst

    Eleven minutes in which the far side of the street becomes a rumour.

    Umbrella: a No. 4
  11. 11

    A Garrow Tuesday

    All of the above, in order, before lunch.

    Umbrella: two

A note from the maker

My great-great-grandfather opened this shop the week the Tolhouse roof first leaked. The roof still leaks; we regard it as advertising.

We make four umbrellas a day, slowly, and the fourth is always the best. Bring us anything with ribs. If it can be mended we will mend it, and if it cannot we will say so kindly and put the kettle on.

Odalys Pluvia

Fifth generation · first daughter

The sign still says Sons. We have had the paint ready since 1998. It keeps raining.
