03

Construction

The round letters are cut from one compass setting. The O is a true circle of radius 356; the G borrows it and stops; the D keeps half and stands it against a wall.

The O is 12 units taller than the H. It has to be, to look the same.

A curve only touches a line at one point; a flat top touches it all the way along. So the circle goes six units over the cap line and six under the baseline. Drawn here with the vertical scale stretched eight times, because at true size you are not supposed to notice.

Spacing

06

Colophon

Halden Grotesk was drawn by Solveig Aamodt at Nordre Type between two winters. It is released, slowly, from a former chandlery on the harbour.

Why the a has one storey

People ask, usually kindly. I drew the o first, and it was right. The a is that o with a wall built against it: the same circle, the same counter, one straight stem cut flat where it stands on the baseline.

A second storey would have been a second opinion. Halden has one fortress and has never felt the need for two.

Solveig Aamodt / Halden / March 2025
